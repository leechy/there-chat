## Full description (max 4000)

There is a messenger for places. Every chat lives at a spot on the map: a queue at the museum, a street concert, a lost umbrella, a Sunday run in the park. Open the map, see what people are talking about around you, and join in.

No account. No phone number. No email.
There doesn't ask who you are. You pick a name for each chat you join, so nobody can tell that two names belong to the same person. Your location is only used on your phone to show the map around you, and it is never sent to our server.

WHAT YOU CAN DO
• Browse chats on the map around you or anywhere you like
• Join a chat with a name of your own, or take a suggested one
• Start a chat at any spot: choose a title, an emoji and where it goes on the map
• Create a channel, where only admins post and everyone else reads
• React to messages and see new ones arrive in real time
• Share a chat with a link, even a hidden one that isn't shown on the map
• Keep the chats you joined in My Chats, with unread counters

YOU STAY IN CONTROL
• Hidden chats: only people with the link can find them
• Approval: decide who can join your chat
• Pre-moderation: approve messages before others see them
• Admins can delete messages and block members of their chats
• Report any message or chat; we review reports within 24 hours
• Delete your own messages, leave chats, or erase all your data in Settings with one tap

There has zero tolerance for abuse. Harassment, hate speech, sexual content and sharing other people's personal information are not allowed. See our Terms of Use: https://there-chat.com/terms/

There is in early beta. We'd love your feedback at support@there-chat.com.
