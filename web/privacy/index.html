Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 8, 2026

There is an anonymous messaging app where chats are tied to places on a map. It is built to need as little data about you as possible: there is no sign-up, and we never ask for your name, phone number, email address or contacts.

This policy explains what data the There apps for iOS and Android and the There service (together, "There") process, and why. There is operated by Andrey Lechev, 16 Groussgaass, L-8297 Olm, Luxembourg ("we", "us"), who is the controller responsible for your data. Questions go to support@there-chat.com.

What we process

  • A random device key. On first launch the app creates a random secret on your device. Our server stores only a one-way hash of it, which lets the server recognise the same device again. The key is kept in your device's secure storage (the Keychain on iOS, Block Store on Android), so it survives reinstalling the app. It is not linked to your identity.
  • What you post. Chats and channels you create (title, emoji and the place on the map you choose), the name you use in each chat, your messages, your reactions, join requests and reports. Chat content is visible to the other members of that chat. Chats marked visible, and their place on the map, can be seen by anyone using the app.
  • Membership data. Which chats your device has joined, your role in them, your notification setting and how far you have read.
  • Push notification token (optional). If you allow notifications, we store the token Apple or Google gives your device so we can send you notifications about chats you follow.
  • Location. If you allow it, the app uses your location on your device to centre the map. Your location is not sent to our server. To load chats, the app sends the area of the map you are looking at, which may or may not be where you are. When you create a chat, the place you pin becomes part of that chat and is public if the chat is visible.
  • Technical data. Like any internet service, our server receives your IP address with each request. We use it to deliver the service and to protect it against abuse, and we do not store it in our database.

We do not use advertising, analytics or tracking tools, we do not build profiles, and we do not sell or share your data for advertising.

Why we process it

We process this data to provide the service you ask for: showing chats on the map, delivering messages and notifications, letting chat admins moderate their chats, and keeping There safe by handling reports and blocking abuse. Where the GDPR applies, the legal bases are performance of our contract with you (Art. 6(1)(b)) and our legitimate interest in running a safe service (Art. 6(1)(f)).

Who else is involved

  • DigitalOcean hosts our server and database in Frankfurt, Germany.
  • Apple (Apple Push Notification service, and Apple Maps on iOS) and Google (Firebase Cloud Messaging and Google Maps on Android) provide notifications and maps. The map providers receive the map area you view, as part of showing you the map. Their own privacy policies apply.

We may disclose data if the law requires us to, for example in response to a valid court order.

How long we keep it

  • Messages stay in their chat until their author or a chat admin deletes them, or the chat is deleted. When a message is deleted, its text is removed from our database.
  • When a chat is deleted, the text of all its messages is removed.
  • Reports are kept as long as needed to handle them and to prevent repeated abuse.

Your choices and rights

  • You can delete your own messages, change your name in a chat, leave chats and turn notifications off at any time in the app. Settings → Erase My Data deletes everything your device posted in one step.
  • You can withdraw location and notification permissions in your device settings.
  • Depending on where you live, you may have the right to access, correct or delete your data, to object to or restrict its processing, and to data portability. Because we do not know who you are, see Delete your data for what to send us so we can find the data that belongs to your device.
  • You can also complain to a data protection authority: the one where you live or work, or the Commission nationale pour la protection des données (CNPD) in Luxembourg, where There is operated.

Children

There is not intended for anyone under 16, and we do not knowingly process data of children under 16.

Changes

If we change this policy, we will update the date above and, for significant changes, tell you in the app.
