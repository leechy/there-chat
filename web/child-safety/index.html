Child Safety Standards

Last updated: November 7, 2026

There has zero tolerance for child sexual abuse and exploitation (CSAE) and child sexual abuse material (CSAM). These standards apply to everything on There: chats, channels, messages, names, chat titles and reports.

What is prohibited

  • Any content that sexualises minors, including text, links and descriptions of child sexual abuse material.
  • Grooming: contacting or befriending a minor in order to sexually abuse or exploit them, online or offline.
  • Sextortion: threatening or pressuring a minor, or anyone, to share sexual images or money.
  • Trafficking or offering minors for sexual purposes, and sharing or asking for ways to find such content.
  • Arranging to meet a minor for sexual purposes, including through the places chats are pinned to.

There is for people aged 16 and over. The app only supports text messages: images, videos and file sharing are not available.

How to report

  • In the app: long-press a message and choose Report, or open a chat's menu and report the whole chat.
  • By email: write to support@there-chat.com with the subject "Child safety". You do not need the app to report.

If a child is in immediate danger, contact your local emergency services first.

What we do

  • We review child safety reports with the highest priority, within 24 hours at the latest.
  • We remove violating content and block the device that posted it from all of There. Chat admins can also delete messages and block members right away.
  • We keep the reported content and related records that law enforcement may need, even after it is removed from the app.
  • We report apparent child sexual abuse material and exploitation to the competent authorities, including the National Center for Missing & Exploited Children (NCMEC) through its CyberTipline and the law enforcement authorities in Germany, where our servers are, and we cooperate with valid law enforcement requests.

Laws

We comply with applicable child safety laws, including the laws of Germany and the European Union.

Child safety contact

Our designated point of contact for child safety, including questions from authorities and from Google Play, is reachable at support@there-chat.com.
