Delete your data

Last updated: November 7, 2026

There has no accounts. We never know your name, email or phone number, so the data we hold is only what your device posted: your messages, reactions, the names you used in chats and which chats you joined.

Erase everything in the app

Open the app, tap the settings button on the map, then Erase My Data and confirm. Right away, our server:

  • deletes the text of all your messages and the names you used in every chat;
  • removes your reactions and your membership in every chat;
  • deletes the chats you created, for everyone, including their title and place on the map;
  • deletes your push notification token and retires your device key.

The app then forgets its key and starts over as a new anonymous user, with nothing linking it to the old one.

Delete single things

  • A message: long-press your message and choose Delete. Its text is removed from our server.
  • A chat membership: open the chat, open its menu and choose Leave Chat.
  • A chat you created: open its Chat Info and choose Delete This Chat. All its messages are removed.

Ask us by email

If you no longer have the app, write to support@there-chat.com with the subject "Delete my data". Because you are anonymous, we need details to find your data:

  • the chats you joined (their titles, or roughly where they are on the map);
  • the name you used in each of them;
  • roughly when you posted, and the text of one or two of your messages.

We will delete the messages and reactions we can match to you, remove your memberships in those chats and, where we can identify your device, its stored key and push token. We reply within 30 days, usually much sooner, and tell you what we deleted. If we cannot match the details to any data, we will tell you that as well.

What is kept

  • Database backups are overwritten within 30 days, so deleted data disappears from them by then.
  • Reports about abuse may be kept for as long as needed to prevent it from happening again, as described in our Privacy Policy.
